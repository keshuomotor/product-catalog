## Technical Information

The catalog provides an overview of our motor series, sizes, and available configurations.

For model-specific information, refer to the relevant product datasheets and mechanical drawings.

Technical specifications may include:

- Rated voltage and current
- No-load speed and operating speed
- Holding torque and output torque
- Step angle and phase resistance
- Gearbox type and reduction ratio
- Motor dimensions and shaft specifications
- Mounting holes and mechanical drawings
- Performance curves, where available

Specifications vary by motor model.

## OEM Motor Options

- Encoders
